Engagement Insights Agent
Insights · Draft outputs for human review

Turn team check-ins into patterns a manager can act on.

Paste a round of team check-in responses. The agent draws out energy patterns, what is motivating and draining the team, the overall sense of belonging, and the conversations a manager should have this week. It works at the level of the team; it does not profile individuals.

Nothing you enter is stored. Outputs are drafts: a person reviews and decides before anything is acted on.
