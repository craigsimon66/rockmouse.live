The Speak-Up Scenario

A workplace learning experience for new managers

You are four months into your first management role. You are sitting in a meeting. A colleague is about to take the blame for something that is not their fault — and you have the data to prove it.

Do you stay silent? Speak up? Ask a question? Wait for a private moment?

Each path leads somewhere different. This scenario lets you explore those paths — and understand what your instincts reveal about how you are learning to lead.

Setting the Scene

Monday Morning. Project Phoenix Status Meeting.

Project Phoenix is a mid-size internal transformation project. Today's status meeting is in the glass-walled boardroom on Level 4. Eight people around the table. The meeting started two minutes late.

Marcus, a peer manager with six years at the company, has just finished presenting. He pauses, then speaks directly: "I want to be transparent. Phoenix is three weeks behind schedule. That's on my team and me. We'll work this weekend to catch up."
