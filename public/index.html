How we build

Six tests every agent has to pass, and six patterns we build from.

The tests are non-negotiable. The patterns are the vocabulary we use to work out what to build next, and in what order.

  • 1
    Human in the loop, alwaysAI drafts. A person reviews and approves before anything reaches a learner.
  • 2
    Context before contentAnchored in your programs, rubrics and capability model, never a generic learner.
  • 3
    Additive, not disruptiveTakes the least rewarding manual work and gives expert time back to judgement.
  • 4
    Data for learning, not surveillanceLearner data stays inside the learning journey, never in performance management.
  • 5
    Progressive complexityOne use case, one context, one output type first. Scale once it earns trust.
  • 6
    Easily contextualisedSwap the industry, tone and evidence base without rebuilding the agent.
